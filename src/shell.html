Built by Paolo Caneparo as a working prototype for the Retail Demand Planner role. Bar names come from Wakuli's public store locator; every volume, stock level and KPI is simulated and carries no real Wakuli data. Forecasts, proposals and KPIs are computed in the browser when the page loads.
